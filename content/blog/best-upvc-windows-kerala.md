Search for the best uPVC windows in Kerala and you'll find a dozen brands, each claiming to be number one. The honest answer is that the *best* window is the one whose profile, glass, hardware **and installation** suit your home, your walls and Kerala's weather. A premium brand fitted badly will leak; a well-chosen window fitted carefully will serve you for years.

We're a Saint-Gobain dealer in Kochi, so we're not neutral about brands — but this guide is about what to check on *any* uPVC window before you sign a quotation.

## Why uPVC suits Kerala homes

Kerala is hard on windows: months of wind-driven monsoon rain, high humidity all year, strong sun, and salty air along the coast. uPVC handles all of it well:

- **It doesn't swell, rot or attract termites** — the problems that finish off timber windows.
- **It doesn't rust or corrode**, even near the sea.
- **It's a poor conductor of heat**, so frames stay cooler than metal on sunny walls.
- **It never needs painting** — an occasional wipe keeps it looking new.

That's why uPVC has replaced timber as the default choice in so many new Kerala homes and renovations.

## What makes a uPVC window "good"?

### 1. The profile

The frame — called the profile — is extruded uPVC with several hollow chambers inside. More chambers and thicker walls generally make a stiffer, better-insulating frame. Ask whether the profile is UV-stabilised for strong sun and made with lead-free stabilisers, and whether larger frames have galvanised steel reinforcement inside. Reinforcement is essential for tall windows and doors.

### 2. Gaskets and drainage

Gaskets — the rubber seals around the shutter and the glass — do the real work against rain and dust. Good windows use continuous gaskets and have drainage slots that carry any water that gets into the frame back outside. In our Kollam splash test, water is thrown straight at a closed window and stays outside: that's gaskets and drainage doing their job.

### 3. The glass

Glass is most of a window's surface, so it decides heat, noise and safety. Don't choose one glass for the whole house — choose by room:

| Room or situation | Glass to ask for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large panes and glazed doors | Toughened | About 4–5× stronger than ordinary glass, breaks into blunt pieces |
| Bedrooms on busy roads | Acoustic laminated, or a double-glazed unit with a laminated pane | Cuts traffic noise |
| West-facing or air-conditioned rooms | Solar-control glass or double glazing | Reduces heat gain |
| Bathrooms | Frosted or patterned | Privacy with daylight |
| Ground floor or security concerns | Laminated | Holds together if struck |

Read more about [toughened glass](/products/toughened-glass), [laminated glass](/products/laminated-glass) and [double-glazed windows](/products/saint-gobain-upvc-windows/double-glazed-windows).

### 4. Hardware

Handles, hinges, rollers and locks are the parts you touch every day. Look for smooth rollers on sliding windows, sturdy hinges or stays on casements, and multi-point locking for doors and ground-floor windows. Near the coast, ask what the hardware is made of and how it is finished.

### 5. Fabrication and installation

This is where most window problems really come from. Corners must be cut and welded precisely, reinforcement fitted where needed, and the frame installed level and square. The gap between frame and wall must be properly sealed, and the sill should slope outward so water runs away. Ask who will install your windows, and what happens if a window leaks after the first monsoon.

> **Tip:** Ask your installer exactly how they will seal the gap between the frame and the wall, and how the sill will shed water. A clear, confident answer is a good sign.

## Which window type for which room?

- **Sliding windows** — [uPVC sliding windows](/products/saint-gobain-upvc-windows/sliding-windows) save space and suit wide openings in living rooms and balconies.
- **Casement windows** — [casement windows](/products/saint-gobain-upvc-windows/casement-windows) open fully for cross-ventilation and seal the tightest, which makes them ideal for bedrooms and exposed walls.
- **French doors** — [uPVC French doors](/products/saint-gobain-upvc-windows/french-doors) open the whole doorway to a sit-out or garden.
- **Fixed glass** — for views and daylight where you don't need ventilation.

Most homes end up with a mix: sliders in the living room, casements in the bedrooms and French doors to the sit-out.

## Wood finish or white?

White is classic and keeps a modern elevation crisp. Wood-grain finishes give the warmth of teak — popular in traditional Kerala homes and villas — without polishing or termite worries; our Kaloor and Kollam projects both use them. See samples in daylight before you decide, because colours look different under showroom lights.

## How much do uPVC windows cost in Kerala?

Prices vary widely because they depend on window size, type (sliding, casement or door), the glass, the finish and the hardware. That's why a per-square-foot rate from a brochure rarely matches the final quotation.

The fair way to compare suppliers is to get quotes for the **same sizes, glass and hardware**, and to check exactly what is included: installation, sealing, transport and after-sales service. ## A quick checklist before you sign

1. Profile: multiple chambers, steel reinforcement where needed, UV-stabilised and lead-free.
2. Continuous gaskets and drainage on every window.
3. The right glass for each room — not one glass for the whole house.
4. Proper hardware and locks for doors and ground-floor windows.
5. Who installs, how the frame is sealed to the wall, and what after-sales support you get.
6. A working sample you have opened and closed yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best uPVC window brand in Kerala?

Several good brands are available in Kerala. Rather than choosing on the name alone, compare the profile quality, glass, hardware, the installer's experience and the after-sales support. We supply Saint-Gobain uPVC windows and are happy to help you compare them with any other quotation.

### How long do uPVC windows last?

Quality uPVC windows that are installed well last for many years with very little maintenance. Clean the frames and tracks occasionally and keep the drainage slots clear.

### Are uPVC windows better than aluminium?

For most rooms in a Kerala home, uPVC insulates better and is easier to look after. System aluminium is better for very large, heavy glass panels and the slimmest frames. Our [uPVC vs aluminium guide](/blog/upvc-vs-aluminium-windows-kerala) compares the two in detail.

### Can uPVC windows replace old windows in an existing house?

Yes. Old timber or steel windows can be replaced with made-to-measure uPVC windows. We survey every opening first and plan the removal of the old frames carefully.
